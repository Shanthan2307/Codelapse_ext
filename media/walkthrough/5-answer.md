# Answer four questions within an hour

Right after you submit, the page prepares **four short questions about your own work**: two about decisions you made while writing it, and two about specific lines of your code.

- Answer each in a few sentences, in your own words. Spelling and grammar don't matter.
- You have **one hour from submitting**. A timer on the page shows the time left.
- Drafts are kept in your browser if you refresh, but you can submit your answers only once.

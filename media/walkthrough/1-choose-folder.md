# Choose your assignment folder

CodeLapse records **only the folder you choose**. Files outside it, and anything in `.git` or `node_modules`, are never recorded.

1. Open the folder for this assignment in VS Code (for example `reverse/`).
2. Run **CodeLapse: Choose Folder to Record**, or click **CodeLapse: Choose folder** in the status bar at the bottom of the window.
3. Pick the assignment folder.

The status bar then shows **CodeLapse: reverse**: recording has started. VS Code remembers the folder, so recording resumes when you reopen it.

Recordings stay on your computer until you export and upload them yourself.
